# Research Ticket

Use when evidence or a decision is the deliverable rather than production
implementation.

```markdown
### Decision or Question

<What must be learned or decided?>

### Context

<Why the answer matters and what is already known.>

### Options or Hypotheses

- <Option or hypothesis>

### Method

<Bounded experiment, measurements, sources, or prototype.>

### Deliverable

<Decision note, benchmark, reproduction, recommendation, or follow-up tickets.>

### Exit Criteria

- [ ] Evidence is recorded and reproducible.
- [ ] A recommendation and trade-offs are documented.
- [ ] Implementation follow-ups are created only when justified.

### Timebox

<Optional explicit limit.>
```

Stop at the exit criteria even when the conclusion is “do not proceed.”
